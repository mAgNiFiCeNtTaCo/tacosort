TacoSort for Windows
====================

To install
  1. Unzip this whole folder first (right-click the zip, "Extract All").
  2. Double-click "Install TacoSort".
     Windows may ask "Do you want to run this file?" because it came from the internet: click Run.
     No administrator password is needed.

What the installer does
  - Copies TacoSort.exe to your own programs folder:
      %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\TacoSort
  - Adds a TacoSort shortcut to the Start menu and to your Desktop.
  - Opens TacoSort.
  It changes nothing else, and after it has finished you can delete this unzipped folder.

To update
  Download the newer zip and run "Install TacoSort" again. Your accounts and settings stay.

To remove
  Open %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\TacoSort and double-click "Uninstall TacoSort".
  Your mail, your folders and your settings are not touched.

Prefer no installer?
  TacoSort.exe in this folder also runs by itself: put it somewhere it can stay and double-click it.
