    What TacoSort does

    It moves mail from shops, apps and companies into folders. Mail from real people stays in your Inbox. Nothing is ever deleted, and Undo puts mail back.

    Not sure what will happen? Press Preview first. It only looks, and moves nothing.

    Look first. Shows where each email would go. Nothing is moved.

    Moves those emails into their folders. Mail from people stays in your Inbox.

    Changed your mind? Puts the emails from the last sort back.

    Looks at the folders you already made, so TacoSort files mail the way you do. Nothing is moved.
